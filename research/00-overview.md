## Key Facts at a Glance

- **4 units** (was 10)
- **Inheritance removed** entirely from required content
- **File I/O added** as required (Topic 4.6, Scanner with File)
- **Recursion: trace-only** — writing recursive methods explicitly excluded
- **MCQ:** 42 questions, 4 choices (A–D), 90 min, **55%** of score
- **FRQ:** 4 questions, 25 total points, 90 min, **45%** of score
- **Exam delivered digitally** in College Board Bluebook (no paper)
- **Big Ideas removed**; 5 Computational Thinking Practices remain
